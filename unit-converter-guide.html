A guide to online unit converters

Convert everyday measurements quickly and understand the differences between common measurement systems.

Length conversions

Length converters help change measurements such as millimetres, centimetres, metres, kilometres, inches, feet and miles. They are useful for DIY projects, travel and everyday measurements.

Weight and mass conversions

Convert between grams, kilograms, milligrams, ounces and pounds. Always check which unit is required before entering a measurement into a form or calculation.

Temperature conversions

Temperature converters commonly support Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin. Unlike simple length conversions, temperature conversions use different starting points and scales.

Speed conversions

Speed converters help change kilometres per hour into miles per hour and metres per second. They can be useful when travelling between countries that use different measurement systems.

Area and volume conversions

Area conversions are useful for land, flooring and room measurements. Volume conversions can help with liquids, containers and cooking measurements.

Digital storage conversions

Data size converters help compare bytes, kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes and terabytes. Be aware that decimal and binary measurement conventions can produce different values.

Check your units carefully

Always check the starting unit and the target unit. A result can be mathematically correct but still unsuitable if the wrong measurement units were selected.

Frequently asked questions

Why use an online unit converter?

It saves time and helps reduce mistakes when converting between different measurement systems.

What is the difference between metric and imperial?

The metric system uses units such as metres and kilograms, while the imperial system includes units such as feet, inches and pounds.

Are online unit conversions accurate?

They can provide accurate mathematical conversions, provided the correct units and conversion factors are used.
