    Guides & frequently asked questions

    Practical tips to help you get more from SmartTools Hub.

    1. How to use online calculators

    Choose the calculator that matches your task, enter the numbers in the labelled fields, then select Calculate. Check that you have entered values in the correct units before using the result.

    • Use the percentage calculator for discounts, increases and proportions.
    • Use the loan calculator for an estimate of regular repayments.
    • Use the age or date difference calculator to compare dates.

    2. Convert measurements accurately

    Converters help change a value from one unit to another. Enter the amount, choose the starting unit and the target unit, and read the converted result.

    • Length and area converters help with everyday measurements.
    • Temperature conversion supports common temperature scales.
    • Data size conversion helps compare bytes and larger units.

    3. Compress and resize images

    Image compression can reduce file size, while resizing changes image dimensions. Start with a copy of your original image and check the result before replacing the original.

    • Use an appropriate output quality to balance clarity and file size.
    • Keep the original if you may need full resolution later.
    • Check the final dimensions and file size before sharing.

    4. Work with PDF files

    Use the PDF tools to combine documents, extract pages, rotate pages or create a PDF from images. Select only the files you intend to process and review the downloaded result.

    • Keep a backup of important documents.
    • Check page order after merging or extracting.
    • Large files may take longer depending on your device.

    5. Make text tasks quicker

    Text utilities can count words and characters, change letter case, remove duplicate lines, find and replace phrases, and reverse text.

    • Paste or type text into the tool's input area.
    • Review the output before copying it elsewhere.
    • Avoid entering confidential or sensitive information.

    6. Use developer utilities

    Developer tools can format JSON, encode or decode URLs and Base64, convert CSV and JSON, and create UUIDs. They are intended as convenient helpers, not a replacement for testing your application.

    • Validate data before using it in production.
    • Do not paste API secrets, passwords or private customer data.
    • Check converted output for formatting and correctness.

    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers about using the website.

    Are the tools free to use?

    The tools on SmartTools Hub are provided for free at present. Some tools may rely on third-party libraries or services to work.

    Do I need to install anything?

    No desktop installation is needed. Open the website in a modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

    Do the tools work on mobile?

    The website is designed to adapt to smaller screens. Very large files or demanding tasks may work better on a computer.

    Are my files uploaded?

    The PDF and image processing tools on this site are designed to process selected files in your browser. The QR code generator sends the text you enter to its external QR service. See the Privacy Policy for details.

    Are my notes and to-do items saved to my account?

    No. Notes and to-do items are currently stored in local browser storage on the device you use and are not synchronised between devices.

    Why might a tool not work?

    Check that your browser is up to date, required fields are filled in and the file format is supported. You can also refresh the page and try again.

    Can I use the results commercially?

    You are responsible for checking the accuracy, permissions and any third-party terms that apply to your intended use. Do not rely on calculator outputs as professional financial, legal or medical advice.

    How can I suggest a new tool?

    Send your idea through the Contact section. Include a short description of what the tool should do.

    Most tools process information directly in your browser. Some features rely on external services, as explained in our Privacy Policy.

    Privacy policy

    Understand what happens when you use SmartTools Hub.

    Last updated: 10 November 2026

    Information and accounts

    If you register or sign in, Supabase processes account information such as your email address and authentication data to provide the account service. Google sign-in is handled through Google's and Supabase's authentication systems.

    Browser-based tools

    Most calculations, conversions and text utilities run in your browser. Image compression and resizing are performed locally in the browser. Notes and to-do items are stored in local storage on your device and are not automatically synchronised to your account.

    QR code service

    The QR code generator sends the text or address you enter to the external QR Server service to create the QR image. Do not submit passwords, confidential information or sensitive personal data.

    Cookies and local storage

    Browser storage is used for local features. Supabase authentication may use browser storage to maintain your sign-in session. Clearing browser storage may remove locally saved notes and tasks and may sign you out.

    Third-party services

    SmartTools Hub is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Supabase for authentication. The QR generator uses QR Server. The PDF tools load the pdf-lib library from jsDelivr; selected PDF and image files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded by these tools. These providers process information under their own policies and terms.
