    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 10 November 2026

    Information and accounts

    If you register or sign in, Supabase processes your email address and authentication information to provide account services. Google sign-in is handled through Google's authentication service.

    Local tools and storage

    Most tools run in your browser. PDF tools process the selected files locally in your browser using the pdf-lib library. Your selected PDF and image files are not uploaded by these tools to a SmartTools Hub server. Notes and tasks are saved in your browser's local storage on that device and are not automatically synchronised with your account.

    QR code service

    The QR code generator sends the text you enter to the external QR Server service to create a QR image. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information into this tool.

    Browser storage

    This website uses local storage for notes and tasks. Supabase authentication may use browser storage to maintain your sign-in session.

    Third-party services

    Supabase provides authentication services, Google provides Google sign-in, QR Server generates QR code images, and jsDelivr delivers the libraries used by this website. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

    Your choices

    You can clear your browser storage to remove locally saved notes and tasks. You can contact us with privacy questions or requests.
