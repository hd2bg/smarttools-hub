How to resize and compress images online

Learn how to make images smaller, change their dimensions and prepare them for websites, documents and sharing.

What is image compression?

Image compression reduces the amount of storage space an image uses. It can make images quicker to upload, download and display on websites. Some compression methods reduce image quality, so always check the finished image.

How to resize an image

Resizing changes an image's width and height. Choose the dimensions you need and, where possible, keep the aspect ratio locked to prevent the image from looking stretched or squashed.

Choose the right image format

JPEG is commonly used for photographs. PNG is useful when transparency or crisp graphics are important. WebP can provide efficient compression for supported websites and browsers.

Prepare images for websites

Use suitable dimensions, avoid unnecessarily large files and give image files descriptive names. Add meaningful alternative text when publishing images on a website.

Keep your original images

Save a copy before making major changes. This allows you to return to the original if the resized or compressed version is too small or loses too much detail.

Frequently asked questions

Does compressing an image reduce quality?

It can. Lossy compression removes some image information, while lossless compression preserves the original image information.

What is the best format for photographs?

JPEG is widely supported and commonly used for photographs. WebP is another useful option for websites.

Why should I resize images?

Resizing can reduce file sizes and help images fit the dimensions needed for websites, documents or social media.
