Use online calculators confidently

Choose a calculator that matches your question, enter values carefully and check units before relying on the result.

Choose the right calculator

Use a basic calculator for arithmetic, a percentage calculator for proportions and percentage changes, a discount calculator for sale prices, and a loan calculator for estimated repayments.

Enter values with the correct units

Check whether a field expects a decimal, percentage, date, currency amount or number of months. Financial estimates depend on the assumptions and values entered.

Check the result

Repeat important calculations independently. Loan estimates may not include fees, taxes, insurance or changes in interest rates.

A simple example

A 20% discount on 50 is 10. Subtract 10 from 50 to get a discounted price of 40.

Frequently asked questions

Are online calculator results always exact?

They depend on the values, formulas and rounding used. Independently verify important results.

Is a loan calculator financial advice?

No. It provides an estimate only; actual lender rates, fees and terms may differ.
