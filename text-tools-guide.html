How to use online text tools

Make everyday writing tasks easier with tools for counting words, editing text, cleaning lists and changing text formats.

Count words and characters

A word counter helps you check the length of an essay, article, application or social media post. Character counts can be useful when a website or form has a text limit.

Change text case

Text case converters can change writing into uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case. They are useful for correcting headings and formatting text consistently.

Remove duplicate lines

Duplicate line removal helps clean lists that contain repeated entries. Review the output afterwards to make sure no important repeated information was meant to remain.

Find and replace text

Find and replace can update repeated words or phrases quickly. Check the replacement carefully, especially when a short word appears inside a longer word.

Format and organise text

Text reversing, word frequency counting and other formatting tools can help with editing, data preparation and basic text analysis.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between words and characters?

Words are groups of text separated by spaces or punctuation. Characters include letters, numbers, spaces and punctuation marks.

Why remove duplicate lines?

Removing repeated entries can make lists easier to read and help prepare data for further processing.

Can text tools help with essays?

Yes. Word counters and text formatters can help with editing and checking length, but you should still proofread your work.
